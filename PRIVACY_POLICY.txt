ABES Attendance Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

ABES Attendance Tracker is an independent student project developed by Rajnish Kumar and is not an official ABES Engineering College application.

Data access: The extension reads attendance information only from the ABES ERP Attendance page that the user is already signed into.

Credentials: The extension does not collect or store ERP passwords or OTPs. Authentication is performed directly on ABES ERP.

Storage: Synced attendance information is stored locally using Chrome extension storage on the user’s browser so the last synced attendance can be viewed later.

Sharing: The extension does not sell, transmit, or share attendance information with the developer or third parties.

Deletion: Users can use “Clear saved attendance” in the extension or remove the extension to delete locally stored extension data.

Permissions: storage is used for local attendance data; activeTab and scripting are used to read attendance from the active ABES ERP attendance page after the user presses Sync Now; host access is limited to https://erp.abes.ac.in/*.
